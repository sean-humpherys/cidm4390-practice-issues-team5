What is a Sprint Retrospective activity?

The Sprint Retrospective is the final event of a Sprint, occurring immediately after the Sprint Review and prior to the next Sprint Planning. While the Sprint Review focuses on inspecting the product increment, the Sprint Retrospective focuses on inspecting the process and the people. It is a dedicated opportunity for the Scrum Team to reflect on how they work together and identify ways to increase quality and effectiveness moving forward.

The entire Scrum Team—including the Scrum Master, Product Owner, and Developers—participates in this meeting. Together, they discuss what went well during the Sprint, what problems were encountered, and how those problems were (or were not) solved. For example, if the developers realized their testing phase was always rushed at the end of the sprint, they might agree to implement a new automated testing rule as an actionable improvement. The most helpful changes identified during the Retrospective are often added to the upcoming Sprint Backlog to ensure continuous improvement.
